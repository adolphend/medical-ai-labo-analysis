# Disease-to-modality evidence matrix

[Imaging research index](README.md) · [Early detection evidence](../diagnostics/06-screening-and-early-detection.md) · [Model reuse registry](model-integration-registry.md)

**Status:** targeted evidence map, not a systematic review. Imaging findings, screening eligibility, and proven early-detection benefits are different claims. No listed AI system is cleared for clinical use by this research.

| Clinical condition / finding | Appropriate modality and context | Evidence for detection / screening | Pretrained or open-source research components | Evidence maturity / gaps |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lung cancer in guideline-eligible high-risk adults | **Low-dose CT** screening | NLST demonstrated lower lung-cancer mortality than chest radiography in its studied high-risk population: [Aberle et al., NEJM 2011](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1102873) | [LUNA16 benchmark](https://luna16.grand-challenge.org/), [MONAI](https://github.com/Project-MONAI/MONAI) | LUNA16 is a nodule benchmark, not a ready validated cancer-diagnosis tool |
| Breast cancer | Screening mammography in indicated groups; targeted ultrasound as adjunct | [USPSTF breast screening recommendation](https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/breast-cancer-screening) (guideline; not a model study) | MONAI plus *task-specific* mammography weights yet to be audited | No particular pretrained checkpoint/clinical safety proven here |
| Diabetic retinopathy | Retinal fundus photography | [Gulshan et al., JAMA 2016](https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2016.17216) developed/validated retinal DL algorithm | MONAI (framework), specialist weights to identify | Original study does not automatically establish open weights or clinical deployment fitness |
| Reduced left ventricular ejection fraction / cardiac dysfunction | Echocardiography, when indicated | [Ouyang et al., Nature 2020](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-020-2145-8) | [EchoNet-Dynamic](https://github.com/echonet/dynamic) | Research video EF estimation, not complete heart-failure diagnosis |
| Fetal anatomy / growth concern | Obstetric ultrasound for protocol-defined scans | [Baumgartner et al., IEEE TMI 2017](https://doi.org/10.1109/TMI.2017.2712367) standard scan-plane recognition | [SonoNet weights](https://github.com/baumgach/SonoNet-weights) | Plane classification ≠ reliable fetal anomaly detection |
| Thyroid nodules | Thyroid ultrasound with clinically appropriate assessment | Task-specific validation required | [MedSAM](https://github.com/bowang-lab/MedSAM) for prompted outlines; specialist models pending | Nodule segmentation alone cannot define malignancy |
| Hydronephrosis / kidney obstruction | Renal ultrasound as clinically indicated | Task-specific validation required | MedSAM interactive annotation; verified kidney model pending | Need clinically standardized grades and multi-site data |
| Gallstones / suspected gallbladder disease | Abdominal ultrasound when indicated | Task-specific validation required | [3D Slicer](https://github.com/Slicer/Slicer) measurements, specialist detectors pending | No ready universal pretrained gallstone detector verified |
| Abdominal aortic aneurysm | Abdominal ultrasound in selected screening/clinical groups | [USPSTF AAA screening recommendation](https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/abdominal-aortic-aneurysm-screening) | MedSAM / manual diameter measurement research | Pretrained AAA inference weights and commercial rights not yet verified |
| DVT | Venous compression/Doppler ultrasound in appropriate pathway | Task-specific evidence audit required | Video and measurement processing; validated checkpoint pending | Vessel visualization alone is not sufficient to diagnose DVT |
| Intracranial hemorrhage / acute stroke evaluation | CT and/or MRI as dictated by emergency pathway | Acute clinical workup, **not** population screening | [MONAI model zoo](https://github.com/Project-MONAI/model-zoo), choose exact task bundle | Avoid implying one generic stroke detector |
| Multiple sclerosis imaging findings | Brain/spine MRI under defined clinical criteria | Disease-specific MRI criteria and external dataset studies needed | [ANTs](https://github.com/ANTsX/ANTs) + task-specific MRI lesion models | MRI lesions are not sufficient by themselves for MS diagnosis |
| Prostate cancer workup | MRI as part of selected diagnostic pathway, often biopsy confirmation | Screening benefit/harm is a separate PSA-based policy debate | MONAI model zoo for appropriate segmentation bundles | MRI prostate analysis ≠ definitive cancer diagnosis |
| Cervical precancer/cancer | HPV DNA testing is a principal screening tool; colposcopy/cytology/pathology as indicated | [WHO cervical screening guidance](https://www.who.int/news/item/06-07-2021-new-recommendations-for-screening-and-treatment-to-prevent-cervical-cancer) | [QuPath](https://github.com/qupath/qupath) for slide research | Do not substitute image-only AI for indicated HPV testing |
| Colorectal cancer / polyps | Stool tests, colonoscopy; CT colonography in selected pathways | Screening trial and guideline review needed by modality | Specialist endoscopic models to audit; [MONAI](https://github.com/Project-MONAI/MONAI) is a framework | No pretrained validated polyp detector identified in current inventory |
| Osteoporosis / vertebral fractures | DXA; radiographs/CT may identify incidental fractures | Screening and opportunistic finding evidence require targeted review | [3D Slicer](https://github.com/Slicer/Slicer), [TotalSegmentator](https://github.com/wasserth/TotalSegmentator) for some anatomy | Quantitative segmentation is not osteoporosis diagnosis |
| Chest findings potentially associated with pneumonia, edema or pleural disease | Chest X-ray with clinical assessment | [Tiu et al., Nature Biomedical Engineering 2022](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41551-022-00936-9) | [CheXzero](https://github.com/rajpurkarlab/CheXzero), [TorchXRayVision](https://github.com/mlmed/torchxrayvision) | CXR label prediction does not establish etiology |

## Evidence grading and future work
- **A:** randomized trial demonstrating patient-relevant outcome benefit for a screening *strategy* (e.g. NLST); does not validate an AI model.
- **B:** external diagnostic performance study; cannot directly establish improved patient outcomes.
- **C:** technical/model publication or test-set benchmark; must check clinical relevance.
- **D:** software framework or hypothesis without verified disease-specific checkpoints.
- Source-specific validation status is more meaningful than assigning a single score to an entire disease.

Expand this matrix with DOI, study population, sensitivity/specificity, prevalence-based PPV, device/site shifts, adverse outcomes, licensed pretrained weights, compute measurements and jurisdiction-specific guidance. Avoid ranking prevalence until epidemiological evidence is compiled.
