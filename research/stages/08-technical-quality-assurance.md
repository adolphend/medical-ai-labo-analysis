## Academic support

Evidence shorthand (W0, W1, W2, W3) is defined in the [central academic references](../evidence/references.md). An academic paper supporting a technology is not evidence of improved patient outcomes.

## Feasible first prototype (no new model training)

DICOM tag consistency, scan quality flags, ECG filtering and repeatable signal measures

## Limitations and verification needs

Quality thresholds require modality-specific clinical evidence; these publications do not validate every proposed QC rule
